# Harr 0.3.13

Harr updates its bundled and installer-pinned LeanCTX to `harr-v3.11.0-unified.10@28d2ab2cf664` on Linux, macOS, and Windows.

LeanCTX build 10 adds the configurable shell inline-compression floor (default 600 tokens), applies the test-runner minimum to successful test output, and preserves explicit compression requests.
